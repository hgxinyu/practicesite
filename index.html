✎ Watch-outs from your practice quiz
  • A fraction slope stays a fraction: −3/2 is not −32.
  • Point-slope signs flip: the point (−3, −4) gives y + 4 = m(x + 3).
  • Standard form: solve for y by dividing every term. 6x + 3y = 6 → y = −2x + 2.
  • Find b by plugging in one point. Check with the other point.
  • A slope of 0 means a flat line: y = b.
